Nandhu Pickles

A complete e-commerce website for a homemade pickles brand. It includes a product showcase, image gallery, pricing and a one-tap WhatsApp ordering flow — so customers can place orders without a complex checkout system.

Project Focus

The site is designed for a small home business: clean product cards, a simple gallery, and a direct WhatsApp order button. No payment gateway needed — customers tap and chat to order.
