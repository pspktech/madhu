MyInstaPro

An Android app to download Instagram videos, reels, photos and stories — fast, free and without a watermark. No login required, no ads, no tracking. Also includes blue tick verification requests and an updates channel.

Project Focus

MyInstaPro is built for speed and simplicity: paste or share an Instagram link, and the app saves the media directly to your device. The interface is clean and focused on one task — downloading.

Built With
